# Round 147 external sources and dependency boundary

Only primary papers, official publisher pages, and the Stacks Project are used for external support.

1. **Stacks Project, Tag 052F, Flattening stratifications.** Used only for the existence and Fitting description of flattening strata for finitely presented modules. It does **not** imply that a stratum or the residual support is smooth.
2. **Stacks Project, Tag 067R, Lemma 31.23.8.** A section of a smooth morphism is a regular immersion. Used in `L-147-03`.
3. **Stacks Project, Tag 0863, Étale localization and strict transform.** Blowup and strict transform commute with étale localization/flat base change. Used in `L-147-02/03`.
4. **Stacks Project, Tags 080X and 0535, Blowing up and flatness.** Used only as background for strict transforms of finite modules; no automatic smooth-center theorem is inferred.
5. Clare D'Cruz, **Integral closedness of MI and the formula of Hoskin and Deligne for finitely supported complete ideals**, arXiv:math/0603211. Used for the complete-ideal Hoskin–Deligne formula on two-dimensional regular local rings.
6. Santiago Encinas and Orlando Villamayor, **Rees algebras and resolution of singularities**, arXiv:math/0702836. Used for Rees algebras, integral equivalence and common permissible transformations.
7. Hiraku Kawanoue, **Toward Resolution of Singularities over a Field of Positive Characteristic, Part I**, arXiv:math/0607009; and Kawanoue–Matsuki, **A New Strategy for Resolution of Singularities in the Monomial Case in Positive Characteristic**, arXiv:1507.05195. Used for the finite-state/idealistic-filtration context and block/eventual-decrease philosophy, not as a proof of arbitrary-dimensional resolution.
8. Round 146 internal theorems `L-146-01--04` and `T-146-01--04`: complete residual point forest, weighted active support, surface finite-Rees compiler and no-rebirth.

## Truth boundary

No external source states `T-147-01` or `T-147-02`. Round 147's new deductions are the FERCC finite certificate, the relative Hoskin–Deligne bundle rank, degree-weighted root splitting, finite-étale orbit-center descent, dormant-subtree no-rebirth, and the three explicit counterexamples. The literature cited here does not prove arbitrary-dimensional positive-characteristic resolution.
